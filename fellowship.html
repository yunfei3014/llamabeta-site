The Investment

Our commitment to every Fellowship company is $2M, invested across pre-seed and seed. It begins with $200K for 3% on a standard post-money SAFE at acceptance; the remaining $1.8M deploys in your future rounds, subject to customary triggering conditions.

Nothing beyond the SAFE. No fees, no additional equity, no program charges. The Fellowship is a benefit of the investment.
